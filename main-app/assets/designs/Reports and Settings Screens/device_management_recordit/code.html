Troubleshooting

01

Device not detected? Ensure the USB cable is securely connected and the SDK driver is running in the background tray.

02

Poor Scan Quality? Clean the sensor surface with a dry micro-fiber cloth. High ambient light can sometimes interfere with ZK9500 sensors.

03

SDK Error? Restart the RecordIT Bridge service from the Start Menu to refresh the biometric socket connection.
